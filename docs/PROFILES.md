# Note Profiles

Profiles change the structure of the output notes.

## course

For course lessons, lectures, and training videos.

Sections: summary, key concepts, detailed notes, takeaways, review questions.

## tutorial

For demos, coding videos, software walkthroughs, and how-to content.

Sections: goal, steps, commands/settings, screenshots, gotchas.

## meeting

For meeting recordings.

Sections: summary, decisions, action items, open questions.

## interview

For interviews and podcasts.

Sections: themes, notable quotes, insights, follow-ups.

## webinar

For webinars, livestream replays, and workshops.

Sections: agenda, main ideas, examples, Q&A, next steps.

## general

For any other video.

Sections: summary, highlights, detailed notes, takeaways.
